Abstract. Industrial motors drive pumps, compressors, fans and conveyors. Unexpected failure stops produc-
tion. This paper presents a practical condition monitoring framework that uses a programmable logic controller
to combine motor electrical current and mechanical vibration. Time and frequency domain features are fused to
classifynormal,warningandcriticalstates. TheworksuitsplantsthatalreadyhavePLCinfrastructureandneed
anaffordablestepfrompreventivetoconditionbasedmaintenance.

Conclusion
A PLC based multi sensor framework for industrial
motor condition monitoring has been presented. It
combines current and vibration evidence into trans-
parentindicatorsandfusesthemthroughsimplePLC
logic.Thedesignusesexistingautomationinfrastruc-
ture,makingitaccessibletofactoriesthatcannotyet
invest in advanced analytics platforms. Future work
shouldvalidatethresholdsonamotortestrigandex-
tend the system with temperature, voltage and speed
information.
